# TotalRecall first-wedge scorecard

Use this after each interview and again after the first six to eight interviews. It turns discovery evidence into a decision record; it does not substitute a weighted spreadsheet for judgment.

## Before scoring

Record the interview in the [buyer interview guide](totalrecall-buyer-interview-guide.md) synthesis-card format. Score only concrete past-workflow evidence. Do not score a prospect's interest in AI, a hypothetical budget, or an unverified market statistic.

## Per-interview evidence card

| Dimension | 0 — absent or adverse | 1 — weak / unclear | 2 — demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recurring pain | The event is rare or no follow-up is expected. | It happens sometimes, but frequency is unclear. | It happens at least weekly or in recurring batches. |
| Material consequence | No meaningful consequence is identified. | General time loss or vague lost revenue. | A concrete missed opportunity, delay, churn risk, or operational loss is described. |
| Relationship context | A generic script and single record suffice. | Some context is useful but not decisive. | The next call depends on prior promises, objections, documents, stakeholders, or timing across sources. |
| Tool gap | Current CRM/cadence/reception workflow works reliably. | Current tools work inconsistently. | The participant describes a specific failure that current tools and people do not solve. |
| Governable call scope | Contact authority or safe content is indeterminate. | A possible scope exists but needs major exceptions. | One limited call reason, contact list, call policy, and human owner can be defined. |
| Measurable pilot | No short-term measure. | A measure exists but needs substantial attribution work. | A 30-day metric and baseline are identifiable. |
| Trust / adoption | Participant would reject any automated calling. | Trust depends on a vague future capability. | Participant names concrete controls that would make a narrowly approved pilot credible. |

**Interview subtotal:** `0–14`. Preserve the rationale and direct quote next to the number; the number alone is not evidence.

## Workflow decision table

Aggregate only evidence from the same workflow. A candidate cannot advance because one enthusiastic participant gave it a high score.

| Gate | Advance requirement | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum sample | Three or more interviews in the same workflow |  |
| Pain | At least three participants report recurring pain and material consequence |  |
| Memory fit | At least three participants need relationship-specific context for the next call |  |
| Safe operating boundary | At least two participants can define an approved contact basis, one call reason, escalation path, and accountable human owner |  |
| Tool gap | At least two participants identify a failure their current stack does not reliably address |  |
| Pilot measurability | At least two participants can state a 30-day metric and a baseline or practical proxy |  |
| Contradictory evidence | No unresolved evidence says generic automation, human-only judgment, or a current tool already handles the job adequately |  |

## Decision outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Advance to a pilot specification** | Every gate is met, with the clearest approved call reason and metric. | Write a one-client, limited-contact pilot specification: timeline inputs, call policy, human approval/escalation, and success measurement. |
| **Run one focused follow-up interview** | The workflow has strong pain and context fit, but one gate is unresolved. | Recruit specifically to resolve that gate; do not broaden the product. |
| **Reject or park** | Pain, context fit, governability, or measurable outcome is not demonstrated. | Preserve the evidence, state the rejection reason, and move to the next hypothesis. |

## Decision record template

```md
### Workflow: [name]

- Interviews: [IDs and roles]
- Date evaluated: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Evidence that supports the workflow:
  - [interview ID — observed fact or short quote]
- Evidence against the workflow:
  - [interview ID — observed fact or short quote]
- Gate results: [pass / unresolved / fail for each gate]
- Decision: [advance / one focused interview / reject or park]
- Decision owner: [name]
- Next bounded action: [specific action]
```

This scorecard works with the [market-scan priorities](totalrecall-market-scan-2026-09-30.md) and the first-wedge rule in [product discovery](totalrecall-product-discovery.md). It deliberately prevents TotalRecall from becoming a generic AI-calling platform before an evidence-backed job is found.
