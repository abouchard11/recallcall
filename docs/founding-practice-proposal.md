::: callout
**One practical patient journey, split into two focused products.** RecallCall
works the overdue-patient list before the visit. Billy Be Good turns the
practice's existing take-home sheet into a private, personalized recovery guide
after the visit.

## The offer at a glance

| Product | When it helps | What it does | Practice outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RecallCall** | Before the appointment | Works the overdue-patient list, handles routine conversation, and advances interested patients toward a booking | More appointments without another front-desk calling block |
| **Billy Be Good** | After the appointment | Converts an approved take-home sheet into a private, day-by-day recovery guide | Clearer aftercare and fewer routine follow-up questions |

## The simple pitch

**RecallCall:** Your recall list is not going to call itself.

Give RecallCall an approved list of overdue patients. It works that list in the
practice's voice, follows the contact rules the practice approves, and sends
ready-to-book conversations back to the team for final confirmation.

**Billy Be Good:** A paper handout becomes a living recovery guide.

Send one existing take-home instruction sheet—even a clear photo. MidnightDev
turns that approved source material into a branded guide that changes with the
patient's procedure date and shows what to do, what may be normal, and when to
call the practice.

::: pagebreak
:::

## Why the two products belong together

RecallCall solves the work practices struggle to complete **before** the visit.
Billy Be Good improves the handoff practices already make **after** the visit.
Neither asks the practice to replace its management system or rewrite its
clinical instructions from scratch.

# Product 1 — RecallCall

## What the practice provides

- A signed Business Associate Agreement before real patient data enters the system
- An eligible patient-recall export with the minimum necessary fields
- The approved contact rules, calling windows, and escalation path
- A short note describing the practice's voice
- Final confirmation of appointment availability

## What RecallCall does

1. **Works the list.** It contacts approved patients according to the practice's campaign rules.
2. **Handles routine conversation.** It answers within the approved scope and keeps interested patients moving.
3. **Hands off the booking.** The practice receives the patients who are ready for a final appointment confirmation.
4. **Reports the work.** The owner receives a concise briefing of contacts, responses, bookings, opt-outs, and projected value.

## What remains human-controlled

- The practice approves the list and campaign rules.
- The practice confirms the final appointment.
- The practice handles clinical, urgent, unusual, or out-of-scope questions.
- The patient can opt out, and that preference must be honored immediately.

## First-month guarantee

If RecallCall books fewer than 10 eligible appointments from the approved recall
list during the first 30 days, the first month is refunded in full.

::: flagbox
**Guarantee language to settle before the pilot:** What counts as an eligible
appointment, how cancellations are treated, the minimum qualifying list size,
and the exact reporting source used to count bookings.
:::

# Product 2 — Billy Be Good

## It starts with what the practice already has

The original Billy Be Good prototype began with a photograph of a veterinarian's
take-home paper form. That same workflow translates directly to dental
aftercare: the practice supplies its approved instructions, and the patient gets
a clearer way to use them.

## The conversion workflow

| Step | Practice input | Patient-facing result |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Capture** | Existing paper handout, PDF, or clear photograph | Source material is organized without changing its clinical meaning |
| **2. Template** | Procedure type, expected timeline, office contact path, approved red flags | A reusable, practice-branded recovery guide |
| **3. Personalize** | Procedure date and minimum necessary patient context | A private link showing the patient's current recovery day |
| **4. Guide** | Practice-approved revisions | Day-specific instructions, normal expectations, and clear escalation guidance |

## Good first templates

- Extraction recovery
- Implant post-operative care
- Root-canal aftercare
- Periodontal treatment home care
- First-week braces or wire guidance
- Clear-aligner onboarding

## Safety boundary

Billy Be Good presents the practice's approved instructions. It does not
diagnose, prescribe, improvise clinical guidance, or replace emergency and
after-hours instructions. Each procedure template must be approved by the
practice before a patient receives it.

::: callout
**The fastest pilot:** Choose one high-volume procedure and send the exact
take-home sheet already used today. Build and approve one template before
expanding to the next procedure.
:::

::: flagbox
**Billy Be Good decisions:** Choose the first procedure template, confirm how
long private links remain active, select the after-hours contact language, and
choose whether pricing is per practice, per procedure template, or bundled into
a higher RecallCall plan.
:::

# Proposed founding-practice pilot

## Phase 1 — Approve the operating rules

- Confirm the qualifying recall list and patient-consent basis
- Complete the vendor, BAA, and data-flow review
- Approve calling windows, contact limits, opt-out behavior, and escalation paths
- Select the first Billy Be Good procedure and approve its source handout

## Phase 2 — Review before launch

- Run a small RecallCall dry run with practice-controlled test contacts
- Review representative conversations and the staff handoff
- Review the Billy Be Good guide on desktop and mobile
- Correct source instructions and escalation language before any patient use

## Phase 3 — Run the 30-day pilot

- Launch the approved recall campaign
- Send Billy Be Good only for the approved procedure template
- Review booking, opt-out, escalation, and aftercare-use signals
- Decide what to keep, revise, or stop at the end of the pilot

| **Billy Be Good add-on** | ==To decide together== | Pricing model should follow the first template's scope and reuse |

## Included in the RecallCall founding pilot

- CSV-based recall-list intake
- Practice-voice configuration
- Approved contact sequence and escalation rules
- Booking-conversation handoff
- Owner briefing and basic outcome reporting
- First-month appointment guarantee, once the final eligibility rules are agreed

## Explicitly outside the initial pilot

- Direct Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve, or other PMS integration
- Automatic calendar writes without staff confirmation
- Insurance eligibility or benefit verification
- New-patient lead generation
- Diagnosis, treatment advice, or emergency triage
- Custom Billy Be Good templates beyond the first approved procedure

## Before real patient use

::: flagbox
The pilot does not accept real patient data until the BAA, vendor review,
minimum-necessary data flow, retention rules, and practice approval are complete.
The public landing page and demo-request form are not patient-intake tools.
:::
